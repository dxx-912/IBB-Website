Who we are

The one-and-only Bruin-led consultancy for global-minded business.

Founded as the first undergraduate international business organization at UCLA, IBB is a tight-knit community united by a passion for shaping the future of a globalized business landscape. With members from all across the world, we promote cross-cultural collaboration and build expertise in global business at UCLA and beyond.

What we do

Consulting built on a genuinely global perspective.

Tapping the perspectives of UCLA's international student body, clients get a global-minded approach to overcoming challenges and finding opportunities — from market entry to growth road mapping, delivered as data-driven insight and strategy they can act on.
