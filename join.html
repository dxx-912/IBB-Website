Before you apply

Questions, answered.

Still not sure about something? Email us at ibbatucla@gmail.com and we’ll answer directly.

IBB is UCLA’s first undergraduate international business organization, and we are open to Bruins from any background. Our current members come from over 30 countries and more than 20 majors — a global outlook matters far more to us than a particular degree.

Applications open and an info session runs on the same day in early October. Applications close a few days later, and shortlisted applicants are invited to coffee chats and then to final interviews the following weekend. The full schedule is above.

Business casual for the info session and coffee chats. Business professional for final interviews.

We anticipate our projects to be around eight weeks long. That timeline is flexible, and we have taken on longer engagements in the past.
